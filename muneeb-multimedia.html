Multimedia basics

So, what is multimedia?

Simply put, it's when different kinds of media team up to get an idea across.

It mixes text, pictures, sound, video and animation 🎨
That makes learning and communicating way more engaging 🙌
You'll find it in education, entertainment, business and websites 🌐
Multimedia basics

The five main building blocks

Tap any one to jump straight to it.

🔤
1 · TEXTWords and characters
🖼️
2 · IMAGESPhotos, drawings, graphics
🎧
3 · AUDIOSpeech, music, sound effects
🎬
4 · VIDEOMoving, real-world visuals
✨
5 · ANIMATIONCreated movement and effects

Put them together and you get a much richer experience.

01
🔤

Text

The written part of any multimedia project.

  • Titles, headings, labels and paragraphs all count.
  • It helps explain ideas clearly and accurately.
  • Think captions, menus, instructions and subtitles.
AaTt123
Example: a title and captions in an educational video.
02
🖼️

Images & Graphics

Still visuals that make information easier to grasp.

  • Photos, illustrations, icons, charts and diagrams.
  • They make content attractive and easier to remember.
  • Common formats: JPEG, PNG and SVG.
PhotoChartIcon
Example: a diagram showing the parts of a computer.
03
🎧

Audio

Sound that adds meaning and emotion.

  • Speech, narration, music and sound effects.
  • It guides attention and keeps a presentation lively.
  • Common formats: MP3, WAV and AAC.
♪ Sound waves ♪
Example: a voice-over explaining a science concept.
04
🎬

Video

A sequence of moving images, usually with sound.

  • Shows real events, demonstrations and stories.
  • Great for tutorials, news, films and online classes.
  • Common formats: MP4, MOV and WebM.
Frame → Motion
Example: a short video demonstrating an experiment.
05
✨

Animation

The illusion of movement, made from images or objects.

  • It can be 2D, 3D or motion graphics.
  • Perfect for explaining things that are hard to film in real life.
  • Used in cartoons, simulations, games and ads.
2D3DMotion
Example: an animated model of the solar system.
